Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. About this site

This website is the personal site of Felipe Alves Martins (felipehere.com). The site itself does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and does not collect personal information from visitors.

2. Access to Google user data

For personal productivity purposes, the site owner authorizes the Hermes personal-assistant application — an OAuth client in a Google Cloud project owned and operated by the site owner — to access data from the site owner's own Google account, including:

  • Gmail (read messages, drafts, send and modify mail)
  • Google Calendar (read and create events)
  • Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs (read and manage files)
  • Google Contacts (read)

3. How this data is used

Data accessed through these permissions is used exclusively for the site owner's personal productivity: reading his own inbox and calendar, drafting communications which he reviews before anything is sent, and scheduling appointments. The data is processed on the owner's personal computer, is never sold, is never shared with third parties, and is never used for advertising or to train machine-learning models.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

The Hermes application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Your rights and revocation

Access to Google user data can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com → Data & privacy → Third-party apps & services).
